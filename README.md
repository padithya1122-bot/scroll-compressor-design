Scroll Compressor Design And Iterations

This repository documents the mechanical design, CAD modeling, iterative prototyping, and engineering development of a scroll compressor component.

Project Overview

The project covers the complete design evolution of a scroll compressor, transitioning from initial mechanical testing hurdles such as shaft misalignment and thermal melting to robust iterative solutions and a final semi-hermetic architecture.

Main Topics And Key Calculation Parameters

Geometry Design
- Scroll Profile: Utilizes a Two-Arc Discharge scroll profile consisting of a Fixed Scroll and an Orbiting Scroll for continuous fluid compression.
- Working Principle: The orbiting scroll moves inside the fixed scroll, progressively reducing pocket volume to raise refrigerant pressure.

Material Selection And Properties (Aluminium 4032 Alloy)
The scroll components are engineered using Aluminium 4032 Alloy, chosen for its favorable thermal and mechanical properties:
- Youngs Modulus E: 73,000 N/mm²
- Yield Strength sigma: 280 N/mm²
- Density rho: 2.69 g/cc
- Poissons Ratio nu: 0.33

Design Evolution And Iterative Improvements

1. Initial Design: Held with an unstable clamp and an Oldhams coupling, which suffered from high-RPM instability and severe shaft misalignment.
2. Second Iteration: Replaced the Oldhams coupling with a more stable jaw coupling and bolted the compressor body directly to the base plank.
3. Third Iteration: Replaced melting plastic sleeves with high-RPM ball bearings to resolve frictional heat generation and tolerance stack-up issues that previously caused components to fuse.
4. Fourth Iteration: Designed a dedicated motor housing casing and incorporated a bottom alignment bar to permanently eliminate lateral and angular misalignment.
5. Semi-Hermetic Model: Fully enclosed the motor inside a sealed housing along with an integrated discharge valve and discharge chamber for advanced operation.
