# Scroll Compressor Design & Iterations

This repository documents the mechanical design, CAD modeling, iterative prototyping, and development of a scroll compressor component[cite: 4].

## 📌 Project Overview
The project covers the complete design evolution of a scroll compressor, transitioning from initial testing hurdles (such as shaft misalignment and thermal melting) to robust iterative solutions and a final semi-hermetic architecture[cite: 4].

### Key Highlights
* **Geometry Design:** Utilizes a Two-Arc Discharge scroll profile (Fixed and Orbiting scrolls) for efficient compression[cite: 4].
* **Material Selection:** Designed using **Aluminium 4032 Alloy**[cite: 4]
  * *Young's Modulus:* 73,000 N/mm²[cite: 4]
  * *Yield Strength:* 280 N/mm²[cite: 4]
  * *Density:* 2.69 g/cc[cite: 4]
  * *Poisson’s Ratio:* 0.33[cite: 4]

---

## 🛠️ Design Evolution & Iterations
1. **Initial Design:** Held with an unstable clamp and Oldham's coupling; suffered from high-RPM instability and severe misalignment[cite: 4].
2. **2nd Iteration:** Replaced Oldham's coupling with a jaw coupling and bolted the compressor body directly to the plank[cite: 4].
3. **3rd Iteration:** Replaced melting plastic sleeves with high-RPM **ball bearings** to resolve frictional heat and tolerance issues[cite: 4].
4. **4th Iteration:** Added a dedicated motor housing casing and a bottom alignment bar to completely eliminate lateral and angular misalignment[cite: 4].
5. **Semi-Hermetic Model:** Integrated the motor inside the sealed casing along with a discharge valve and discharge chamber[cite: 4].
