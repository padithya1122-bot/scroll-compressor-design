Scroll Compressor Design And Iterations

This repository documents the mechanical design, CAD modeling, iterative prototyping, and development of a scroll compressor component.

Project Overview

The project covers the complete design evolution of a scroll compressor, transitioning from initial testing hurdles such as shaft misalignment and thermal melting to robust iterative solutions and a final semi hermetic architecture.

Key Highlights

* Geometry Design: Utilizes a Two Arc Discharge scroll profile featuring fixed and orbiting scrolls for efficient compression.
* Material Selection: Designed using Aluminium 4032 Alloy
  * Youngs Modulus: 73000 N per mm square
  * Yield Strength: 280 N per mm square
  * Density: 2.69 g per cc
  * Poissons Ratio: 0.33

Design Evolution And Iterations

1. Initial Design: Held with an unstable clamp and an Oldhams coupling, which suffered from high RPM instability and severe misalignment.
2. Second Iteration: Replaced the Oldhams coupling with a jaw coupling and bolted the compressor body directly to the base plank for better stability.
3. Third Iteration: Replaced melting plastic sleeves with high RPM ball bearings to successfully resolve frictional heat and tolerance issues.
4. Fourth Iteration: Added a dedicated motor housing casing and a bottom alignment bar to completely eliminate lateral and angular misalignment.
5. Semi Hermetic Model: Integrated the motor inside a sealed casing along with a discharge valve and a discharge chamber for advanced operation.
